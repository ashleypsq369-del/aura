## 🎓 LESSONS LEARNED

### Research is Critical
- Understanding actual palliative care needs prevented generic groups
- Ghana-specific research led to culturally appropriate support
- Evidence-based approach ensures comprehensive coverage

### Categorization Matters
- 10 clear categories help users find relevant groups
- Balance between specificity and overlap
- Multiple entry points for same need (e.g., cancer in condition-specific, faith in spiritual)

### Privacy Enables Sharing
- Anonymization removes barriers to authentic sharing
- Particularly important for stigmatized conditions
- Users more likely to engage when identity protected

### Cultural Context is Key
- Ghana-specific groups not just translation
- NHIS, traditional practices, rural access are real needs
- Language (Twi) and culture deeply matter
