## ✅ HOW TO ACCESS (2 WAYS)

### METHOD 1: From Patient Page (EASIEST)

```

3. Click on any patient (e.g., "John Mensah")
```

## 🎤 VOICE INPUT

**Where:** Brief Pain Inventory form only (so far)

**How to find it:**
```
1. Go to assessments dashboard
2. Click [⚡ BPI] card
3. Look for blue microphone buttons (🎤)
   - Next to "Pain Location" textarea
   - Next to "Notes" textarea
4. Click microphone, speak, text appears!
```

---

## 📴 OFFLINE MODE

**Where:** Bottom-right corner of screen

**When it appears:**
- When you go offline (disconnect WiFi)
- When you have pending assessments to sync

**What it looks like:**
```
┌─────────────────────┐
│ 📴 Offline Mode [2] │
│ 2 pending           │
└─────────────────────┘
```

## ❓ TROUBLESHOOTING

### "I don't see the button"

1. **Refresh your browser** (Ctrl+R or F5)
2. **Clear cache** (Ctrl+Shift+R)
3. **Check you're logged in** as nurse or caregiver
4. **Check the patient detail page** (not dashboard)
