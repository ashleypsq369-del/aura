## 1. Introduction

**ProjectAura** ("we," "us," "our") is an AI-powered palliative care management platform designed for healthcare delivery in Ghana and Sub-Saharan Africa. We understand that the information stored on this Platform is among the most sensitive data anyone can entrust to a digital service - health records, end-of-life wishes, personal memories, and family communications.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, protect, and manage your personal and health information when you use ProjectAura (the "Platform").

**Our Privacy Commitments:**
- We collect only the data necessary to deliver palliative care services
- We never sell your personal or health data
- We use encryption to protect your data in transit and at rest
- You have rights to access, correct, and delete your data
- We are transparent about how AI systems use your data
- We comply with HIPAA, GDPR, and the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

Under GDPR, we process your data under the following lawful bases:

### 4.1 Consent (Article 6(1)(a))
- You explicitly consent to data collection and processing when creating an account
- You consent to AI/ML analysis of your data for care improvement
- You consent to communication via email, SMS, and in-app notifications
- You can withdraw consent at any time

### 4.2 Vital Interests (Article 6(1)(d))
- Processing health data necessary to protect the vital interests of patients receiving palliative care
- Alerting clinicians to critical vital signs or symptom changes

### 4.3 Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c))
- Retaining medical records as required by healthcare regulations
- Responding to legal requests and court orders
- Complying with regulatory audit requirements

### 4.4 Public Interest (Article 6(1)(e))
- Contributing to public health research using de-identified data
- Supporting palliative care quality improvement

### 4.5 Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f))
- Improving Platform functionality and AI models
- Security monitoring and fraud prevention
- Generating aggregated analytics

### 4.6 Special Category Data (Article 9)
Health data is special category data under GDPR. We process it under:
- **Explicit consent** (Article 9(2)(a))
- **Vital interests** where consent cannot be given (Article 9(2)(c))
- **Public health** purposes (Article 9(2)(i))
- **Healthcare purposes** under professional obligation (Article 9(2)(h))

### 6.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We share limited data with these third-party services to operate the Platform:

#### Email Delivery - SendGrid
- **Data shared**: Recipient email address, email subject and body
- **Purpose**: Sending notifications, reminders, alerts
- **Legal basis**: Legitimate interest (service delivery)
- **Safeguards**: SendGrid processes data under their privacy policy and DPA

#### SMS Delivery - Twilio
- **Data shared**: Recipient phone number, message content
- **Purpose**: Sending medication reminders, appointment alerts, critical notifications
- **Legal basis**: Consent and vital interests
- **Safeguards**: Twilio is GDPR compliant and processes data under DPA

#### AI/LLM APIs - Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), Groq
- **Data shared**: De-identified or minimally identified query text for chatbot responses
- **Purpose**: Generating AI chatbot responses, care recommendations
- **Legal basis**: Consent and legitimate interest
- **Safeguards**: 
  - We minimize data sent to LLM APIs (no full medical records sent)
  - API providers do not use Platform data to train their own models

  - Queries are scoped to the specific interaction only

#### Database Hosting - Neon (PostgreSQL)
- **Data shared**: All Platform data (encrypted at rest)
- **Purpose**: Primary data storage and retrieval
- **Safeguards**: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.2+ in transit, access controls

#### Application Hosting - Render
- **Data shared**: Application code and runtime data
- **Purpose**: Hosting the FastAPI backend application
- **Safeguards**: Secure deployment, environment variable management, no direct database access

#### File Storage
- **Data shared**: Uploaded files (photos, videos, legacy content, documents)
- **Purpose**: Storing user-uploaded media and documents
- **Safeguards**: Access-controlled storage, encrypted when at rest

## 8. Data Security

### 8.1 Encryption
- **In Transit**: All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses TLS 1.2+ encryption
- **At Rest**: Database and file storage encrypted with AES-256
- **Passwords**: Hashed using bcrypt (never stored in plain text)
- **Tokens**: JWT authentication tokens with expiration

### 8.2 Access Controls
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Users can only access data appropriate to their role
- **Authentication**: JWT-based authentication required for all API access
- **Authorization**: Per-endpoint authorization checks
- **Account Lockout**: Accounts lock after multiple failed login attempts
- **Session Management**: Tokens expire and must be refreshed

### 8.3 Audit Logging
We maintain comprehensive audit logs recording:
- Who accessed which patient records
- What actions were taken (create, read, update, delete)
- When actions occurred (timestamps)
- From what IP address
- Audit logs are tamper-evident and retained for compliance

### 8.4 Network Security
- **CORS**: Configured to allow only authorized origins
- **Input Validation**: All API inputs validated using Pydantic schemas
- **SQL Injection Prevention**: SQLAlchemy ORM with parameterized queries
- **Rate Limiting**: API endpoints rate-limited to prevent abuse
- **Secure Headers**: HTTP security headers configured

### 8.5 Physical Security
- Cloud infrastructure providers maintain SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 certifications
- Data centers with 24/7 physical security, biometric access controls
- Redundant power, cooling, and network connectivity

### 8.6 Breach Notification
In the event of a data breach:
- We notify affected users within 72 hours of discovery (GDPR and Ghana DPA requirement)
- We notify the Ghana Data Protection Commission within 72 hours
- We notify relevant supervisory authorities as required
- Breach notifications include: nature of breach, data affected, measures taken, recommended actions

## 9. AI Transparency and Your Rights

### 9.1 How AI Uses Your Data

ProjectAura uses AI/ML in several ways. We are committed to transparency:

#### Risk Prediction (XGBoost)
- **Input**: Your vitals, symptoms, assessment scores, medications
- **Output**: Risk score (0-100), risk level, care pathway recommendation
- **Explanation**: SHAP values show which factors influenced the prediction
- **Feedback**: You can flag predictions as inaccurate to improve the model
- **Human Oversight**: Clinicians review all AI predictions before acting

#### Chatbot (LLM-powered)
- **Input**: Your questions and conversation context
- **Output**: Text responses, care suggestions, resource recommendations
- **Processing**: Queries sent to Anthropic/Google/Groq APIs (minimally identified)
- **Storage**: Conversation history stored for context and improvement
- **Crisis Detection**: System flags messages indicating self-harm or crisis for clinician escalation

#### Personalization Engine
- **Input**: Your preferences questionnaire responses
- **Output**: Tailored content, resource recommendations, community suggestions
- **Purpose**: Reducing information overload and increasing relevance

### 9.2 Your AI Rights
- **Right to Explanation**: You can request an explanation of any AI-generated prediction about you
- **Right to Human Review**: You can request human review of AI decisions
- **Right to Object**: You can object to AI processing of your data (though this may limit some features)
- **Right to Feedback**: You can provide feedback on AI accuracy to improve or correct outputs
- **No Automated Decision-Making**: AI does not make autonomous decisions about your care - all decisions involve human clinicians

### 9.3 AI Data Minimization
- We send only the minimum necessary data to LLM APIs
- We do not send full medical records to third-party AI services
- AI training on Platform data uses de-identified, aggregated information
- You can opt out of AI data use for model improvement (contact privacy@projectaura.com)

- Your access is revoked immediately
- Your profile data is deleted (after 90-day grace period)

## 14. Digital Legacy and Memory Vault Privacy

### 14.1 Consent Framework
- Patients must provide explicit, written consent before creating digital legacy content

### 14.3 Content Privacy

### 14.4 Bereavement Entries
- Bereavement journal entries can be private or shared
- Sentiment analysis runs on entries to detect grief stages and distress
- If crisis indicators are detected, appropriate support resources are suggested
- Entries are never shared without the author's explicit consent
