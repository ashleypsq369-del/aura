## 🎓 UNDERSTANDING SHAP

```
Feature: Pain Level Avg
Value:   8.2/10
SHAP:    +0.35 (+35%)
Impact:  ↗️  INCREASES RISK
Why:     "Severe pain significantly impacts wellbeing"

Legend:
  ↗️  Red Arrow   = INCREASES risk (bad)
  ↘️  Green Arrow = DECREASES risk (protective)
  +X% = Contribution to overall risk
  #1, #2, #3 = Importance ranking
```
