- **Adapts content:** Simple vs detailed explanations
- **Filters resources:** Based on patient interests

- **Content changes per disease:** Cancer sees cancer protocols only
- **Priority sorted:** Critical issues first
- **Evidence-based:** WHO guidelines, clinical best practices
