## 💡 Quick Tips

### For Clinicians:
1. **Brain icons mean "concerning"** - Click to understand why
2. **Feedback helps everyone** - Every submission improves the system
3. **Trust but verify** - XAI is decision support, not replacement
4. **Check recommendations** - Evidence-based interventions suggested

### For Administrators:
1. **Monitor feedback stats**: `/api/comprehensive-ai/focused-explanation-feedback/stats`
2. **Track helpfulness**: Target >80% helpful
3. **Review weekly**: Identify low-performing explanations
4. **Plan ML training**: After collecting 2-4 weeks of data

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Issue: "No brain icons appear"
**Solution**: Brain icons only appear when scores are concerning (usually <6 or >threshold)

### Issue: "XAI button does nothing"
**Solution**: Check browser console. Backend might not be running. Restart Terminal 13.

### Issue: "Feedback not submitting"
**Solution**: Check network tab. Ensure backend is accessible at localhost:8000

### Issue: "Assessment type not recognized"
**Solution**: Check spelling. Use exact names from list above.
