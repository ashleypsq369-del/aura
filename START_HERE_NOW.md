## 🔍 SHAP EXPLANATIONS EXPLAINED

**Example**:
```
#1  Pain Level Avg: 8.2/10
    ↗️  +35% (increases risk)
    "Severe pain significantly impacts wellbeing"
```

**What it means**:
- **#1** = Most important factor
- **8.2/10** = Patient's actual pain level
- **+35%** = Increases overall risk by this much
- **↗️ Red** = This is BAD (risk factor)
- **Plain English** = Why it matters

**Green arrows ↘️** = GOOD (protective factors)
