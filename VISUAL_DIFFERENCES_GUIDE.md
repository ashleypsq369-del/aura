## 🎯 QUICK IDENTIFICATION GUIDE

### How to Spot AI Features:

1. **Look for PURPLE color scheme**
   - Purple headers
   - Purple gradients
   - Purple cards
   - Purple badges

2. **Look for "🧠 AI-POWERED" badge**
   - Animated badge
   - Yellow background with purple text
   - Indicates ML/AI functionality

3. **Look for patient-specific content**
   - Patient name displayed
   - Disease mentioned
   - "Personalized" or "Adaptive" in title

4. **Look for metrics/scores**
   - Workload: 45/100
   - Risk: 87%
   - Confidence: 92%
   - Indicates algorithmic calculation

5. **Look for "Based on..." reasoning**
   - "Based on Cancer profile..."
   - "AI Reason: ..."
   - "Factors: ..."
   - Indicates intelligent analysis
