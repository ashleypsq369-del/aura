## 8. Digital Legacy and Bereavement Features

### 8.1 Digital Legacy Consent
The Platform allows patients to create digital legacies (memories, messages, photos) for delivery to designated heirs after death. Use of these features requires:
- Explicit written consent from the patient
- Designation of heirs with relationship information
- Specification of what content can be accessed
- Optional witness signature
- Understanding that consent can be revoked at any time before death

### 8.2 Heir Access
After a patient's death is recorded in the system:
- Designated heirs are notified of their access
- Heirs can view content assigned to them based on patient preferences
- Access levels respect patient's privacy wishes (e.g., medical records vs. personal memories)
- Heirs cannot modify original content

### 8.3 Bereavement Support
The Platform provides:
- Grief resources and educational materials
- Peer support forums for bereaved family members
- Memory preservation tools
- Professional referrals for grief counseling

### 8.4 Content Moderation
All user-generated content (posts, memories, messages) is subject to moderation to ensure compliance with community standards and prevent harmful content.
