## 📊 COMPLETE SYSTEM INVENTORY

### **🏥 CLINICAL MANAGEMENT (10 Major Features)**

1. **Patient Management**
   - Full patient profiles with demographics
   - Ghana-specific fields (NHIS, Ghana Card)
   - Primary diagnosis & secondary conditions
   - Care pathway assignment (cancer, cardiac, renal, respiratory, liver, HIV, general)
   - Risk scoring & risk levels
   - Advance directives & DNR status
   - Emergency contacts
   - Facility & region tracking
   - Status management (active, critical, discharged, deceased)

2. **Vital Signs Monitoring**
   - Heart rate, BP (systolic/diastolic)
   - Oxygen saturation
   - Temperature, respiratory rate
   - Weight, blood glucose
   - Manual & device integration
   - Automated flagging for critical values
   - Historical tracking & trends
   - Real-time alerts for abnormal readings

3. **Medication Management**
   - Prescription tracking (name, dose, frequency, route)
   - PRN (as-needed) medication support
   - Start/end dates
   - Discontinuation tracking with reasons
   - Adherence logging
   - Scheduled dose reminders (15 min before)
   - Missed dose detection (30 min after)
   - Drug interactions checking
   - Prescriber tracking

4. **Symptoms Tracking**
   - Pain assessment (location, level 1-10)
   - Fatigue, nausea, anxiety levels
   - Appetite, sleep quality
   - Breathlessness, confusion
   - Mood tracking
   - Trend analysis
   - Multi-symptom correlation

5. **Clinical Assessments (12 Validated Tools)**
   - ESAS (Edmonton Symptom Assessment System)
   - PPS (Palliative Performance Scale)
   - MMSE & MoCA (Cognitive assessments)
   - PHQ-9 (Depression screening)
   - GAD-7 (Anxiety screening)
   - Barthel Index (Activities of Daily Living)
   - Brief Pain Inventory
   - Quality of Life Assessment (FACT-G style)
   - Zarit Burden Interview (Caregiver burden)
   - Motor Skills Assessment
   - Each tool with proper scoring & interpretation

6. **Appointments**
   - Scheduling with clinicians
   - Multiple types (consultation, follow-up, treatment, etc.)
   - Location tracking (facility, home visit, telehealth)
   - Status management (scheduled, completed, cancelled, missed)
   - Automated reminders (24h & 1h before)
   - Cancellation tracking with reasons
   - Completion notes
   - Duration tracking

7. **Care Plans**
   - Goals of care
   - Pain management plans
   - Psychological support strategies
   - Spiritual care plans
   - Family support plans
   - Advance directives notes
   - Bereavement planning
   - Regular review scheduling
   - Version control

8. **Alerts System** 🚨
   - Critical vitals alerts (O2 < 90%, HR abnormal, BP extremes, Temperature)
   - Patient deterioration warnings
   - Fall incidents
   - Severe pain alerts
   - Equipment failures
   - RED AlertCircle icon with pulsing badge
   - Clinical staff only visibility
   - Acknowledgment tracking
   - Navigation to patient details

9. **Clinical Notes**
   - Free-text clinical documentation
   - Note types (progress, assessment, consultation, etc.)
   - Author tracking
   - Private/shared visibility
   - Timestamped entries
   - Patient context

10. **Intelligent Care System** 🤖
    - AI-powered risk prediction
    - XGBoost ML model
    - SHAP values for explainability
    - Plain-language recommendations
    - Proactive monitoring (every 5 minutes)
    - Automated alert generation
    - Feature importance analysis
