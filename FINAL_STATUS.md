## 🎓 Key Learnings

### What Works
1. **Teal/Cyan palette** - Perfect for healthcare
2. **2px borders** - Clear visual separation
3. **Gradients** - Professional without overdoing it
4. **Shadows** - Proper depth hierarchy
5. **Animations** - Engaging feedback

### Best Practices
1. Always use border-2
2. Combine shadows with hover
3. Use gradients sparingly
4. Keep rounded corners consistent
5. Add transitions to everything
