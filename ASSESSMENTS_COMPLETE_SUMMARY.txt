───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  10 VALIDATED ASSESSMENT TOOLS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. ESAS (Edmonton Symptom Assessment System)
   • 9 symptoms (pain, tiredness, nausea, etc.)
   • 0-10 scale for each
   • Most widely used in palliative care
   • Daily symptom tracking

2. PPS (Palliative Performance Scale)
   • Functional status 0-100%
   • 5 domains: ambulation, activity, self-care, intake, consciousness
   • Strong prognostic value
   • Modified from Karnofsky Scale

3. MMSE & MoCA (Cognitive Assessment)
   • Memory and cognition testing
   • MMSE: 30 points, since 1975
   • MoCA: More sensitive for mild impairment
   • Detects cognitive decline

4. PHQ-9 (Depression Screening)
   • 9 questions, 0-3 scale each
   • Total 0-27
   • Validated depression screening
   • Includes suicide risk question

5. GAD-7 (Anxiety Screening)
   • 7 questions, 0-3 scale each
   • Total 0-21
   • Validated anxiety screening
   • Quick 2-3 minute administration

6. Barthel Index (Activities of Daily Living)
   • 10 ADL items
   • Total 0-100
   • Independence assessment
   • Used since 1965

7. Brief Pain Inventory
   • Pain severity (4 items)
   • Pain interference (7 items)
   • Location and type
   • Medication effectiveness

8. FACT-G (Quality of Life)
   • 4 domains: Physical, Social, Emotional, Functional
   • 27 questions total
   • 0-108 score
   • Cancer-specific QOL assessment

9. Zarit Burden Interview
   • Caregiver burden assessment
   • 12 questions (short form)
   • 0-48 scale
   • Identifies need for respite care

10. Motor Skills & Physical Function
    • Grip strength test
    • Timed Up and Go (TUG)
    • 6-minute walk test
    • Balance and coordination
    • Fall risk assessment
