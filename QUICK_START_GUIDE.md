### **2. DRAGGABLE INTERFACE** 🎯

#### **How to Drag Buttons:**
1. Find the 🤖 (Blue) or 🧠 (Teal) button
2. Click and HOLD
3. Move mouse to desired position
4. Release to drop
5. Single-click (no drag) = Opens chat

#### **How to Drag Windows:**
1. Open ChatBot or AI Assistant
2. Click and HOLD the header (colored bar at top)
3. Move window anywhere
4. Release to place

#### **Close Windows:**
- Click the ❌ button in header, OR
- Click outside the window (on the dark backdrop)

## 💡 QUICK TIPS

### **Try Dark Mode:**
1. Go to Settings → Appearance
2. Select "🌙 Dark Mode"
3. ✅ Entire app turns dark immediately!

### **Disable AI Thinking Animation:**
1. Go to Settings → AI Tools
2. Find 🧠 AI Assistant section
3. Toggle OFF "Show Thinking Process"
4. ✅ AI responds instantly without animation!

### **Enable Auto-Navigation:**
1. Go to Settings → AI Tools
2. Find 🧠 AI Assistant section
3. Toggle ON "Auto-Navigate"
4. Ask AI: "Take me to patients"
5. ✅ Automatically navigates after 1 second!

### **Increase Font Size:**
1. Go to Settings → Accessibility
2. Select "Extra Large" from Font Size
3. ✅ All text gets bigger!

### **Drag the AI Buttons:**
1. Click and HOLD on 🤖 or 🧠 button
2. Move to corner you prefer
3. Release
4. ✅ Button stays there during session!

## 🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING

### **Problem: Settings don't save**
**Solution:** 
- Check browser localStorage is enabled
- Try clearing browser cache
- Refresh the page

### **Problem: Theme doesn't change**
**Solution:**
- Hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
- Check if Auto mode is selected (uses system preference)

### **Problem: Can't drag buttons**
**Solution:**
- Click and HOLD for 5 pixels of movement
- Don't just click - must drag
- Try refreshing the page

### **Problem: AI Assistant not navigating**
**Solution:**
- Check if "Auto-Navigate" is ON in Settings → AI Tools
- If OFF, click the "Go to Page" button manually
- Verify you're asking for a valid page
