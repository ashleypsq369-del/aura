## 🔍 SHAP EXAMPLE

```
#1  Pain Level Avg: 8.2/10
    ↗️  +35% (increases risk)
    "Severe pain (8.2/10) significantly impacts 
     wellbeing and requires urgent attention"
```

**What it means**:
- **#1** = Most important factor
- **8.2/10** = Patient's actual data
- **+35%** = Contribution to risk
- **↗️ Red** = Increases risk (bad)
- **↘️ Green** = Decreases risk (protective)
