## ✨ Try This First

### Add Your First Contact:

1. **Click** "Add Contact" button (top right, teal)
2. **Type** "Dr" or any name in search box
3. **See** search results appear
4. **Click** "Add Contact" on someone
5. **See** toast notification: ✅ "Contact added successfully!"
6. **See** contact appear in list

### Star as Favorite:

1. **Find** your contact in the list
2. **Click** the star icon (☆)
3. **See** star turn yellow (★)
4. **See** contact move to "Favorites" section

### Open Chat:

1. **Click** anywhere on the contact card
2. **See** automatic switch to Chats tab
3. **See** conversation open
4. **Type** a message and send!
